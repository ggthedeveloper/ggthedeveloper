<h3 id="about-me">⚡ About Me</h3>

> **"Building intelligent, dependable systems at the convergence of AI research, full-stack software engineering, and real-world infrastructure."**

I am a **B.Tech Computer Science student at SRM University, AP**, with hands-on experience spanning applied machine learning, deep learning research, and end-to-end full-stack software development.

- 🔨 **What I Build**: Complete, production-grade applications that solve tangible problems — from meteorological AI post-processing dashboards and railway asset optimization engines to browser-based 3D simulations.
- 🔬 **What I Research**: Efficient deep learning and model compression. Specifically investigating **Transformer model compression**, **CoFi structured pruning**, and parameter-efficient fine-tuning via **QA-LoRA (Quantization-Aware Low-Rank Adaptation)** to make large models viable on resource-constrained hardware.
- 📚 **What I Am Learning**: Advanced ML systems, high-throughput model inference, distributed systems, and scalable backend architecture.
- 🎯 **The Engineer I Am Becoming**: A disciplined systems and AI engineer who values empirical validation, algorithmic rigor, and clean architectural design over hype and superficial claims.
